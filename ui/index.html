    Import from Persistwise web

    A one-time move: bring your feeds, posts, Wayback and IPFS links and saved copies from your Persistwise web account onto this computer. After that, Persistwise checks your feeds itself.

    Make the export with the one-time script tools/web-export/export_archive.rb (see docs/web-migration.md), then choose the .zip here. Importing a fresher export again later updates what changed, with no duplicates.

    Settings

    Wayback Machine

    Persistwise asks the Internet Archive's free, public Save Page Now to capture pages you save. No account is needed. The page's address is sent to the Internet Archive and the capture becomes public. Save Page Now is often busy; when it fails, your local copy is still saved and you can try again from the page.

    Feeds

    Persistwise checks the feeds you follow and saves every new post, just like the Persistwise website did, but on this computer.

    IPFS (optional)

    IPFS keeps a copy addressable by its content (a CID) so anyone can fetch it from any IPFS gateway while someone pins it. It is entirely optional. Persistwise does not run or pay for pinning; you choose one of these, or leave it off.

    Keys are stored only on this computer, in your Persistwise data folder (readable only by you), and are never shown again in full.

    About Persistwise Desktop

    A personal web archive that runs entirely on your own computer. No account, no Nine Point Labs servers, no telemetry. The only network requests it makes are fetching the pages you save, plus the Wayback Machine and IPFS backups if you turn them on.

    Linux: add to your app menu

    If you run the AppImage without an integrator, start it once from a terminal with --install-desktop-entry to add Persistwise to your app menu and dock (and --remove-desktop-entry to take it out again). Gear Lever and AppImageLauncher also work.
